## Printable exercise PDFs

- `vendor/pdf/pdf-lib-1.17.1.min.js`: [pdf-lib 1.17.1](https://github.com/Hopding/pdf-lib), distributed under MIT; the upstream license is included as `vendor/pdf/pdf-lib-LICENSE.md`.
- `vendor/pdf/fontkit-1.1.1.umd.min.js`: [@pdf-lib/fontkit 1.1.1](https://github.com/Hopding/fontkit), the MIT-licensed fork of [fontkit](https://github.com/foliojs/fontkit). The fork's README declares MIT; the upstream fontkit license is included as `vendor/pdf/fontkit-LICENSE.md`.
- `assets/fonts/NihongoLatin-Regular.ttf` and `NihongoJapanese-Regular.ttf`: static, renamed subsets of [Noto Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosans) and [Noto Sans JP](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansjp), retrieved from Google Fonts on 2026-11-09. Licensed under SIL OFL 1.1; complete upstream copyright/license texts are included as `assets/fonts/OFL-Latin.txt` and `OFL-JP.txt`.

The font subsets are generated by `scripts/prepare-pdf-fonts.py` from upstream `NotoSans[wdth,wght].ttf` and `NotoSansJP[wght].ttf`, using `fonttools`. The Latin subset retains Vietnamese, Latin transcription marks and punctuation. The Japanese subset retains kana, punctuation and the characters used by the exercise sources. To regenerate, install fonttools in a maintenance environment, download those upstream font files and run:

```sh
python scripts/prepare-pdf-fonts.py --latin /path/to/NotoSans.ttf --japanese /path/to/NotoSansJP.ttf
node scripts/check-exercises-pdf.mjs
```

These assets are loaded from the website itself on demand; generating a worksheet does not contact a font CDN or an external PDF service.

## Handwriting model

`hiragana-model.js` is a neural network trained on the stroke data listed below (Tomoe, KanjiVG, KanjiCanvas) and on glyphs rendered from Japanese fonts distributed through [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/). The model weights are distributed under the terms of the data they derive from. Handwriting drawn in the app stays in the learner's browser.

Fonts used for training: Klee One, Yomogi, Zen Kurenaido, Hachi Maru Pop, Yusei Magic, Kiwi Maru, Zen Maru Gothic, Mochiy Pop One, Darumadrop One, Potta One, RocknRoll One, Reggae One, New Tegomin, Slackside One, Yuji Syuku, Yuji Boku, Yuji Mai, Zen Antique, Sawarabi Gothic, Sawarabi Mincho, M PLUS Rounded 1c, Kaisei Opti, Kaisei Tokumin, Shippori Mincho, Noto Sans JP, Noto Serif JP, BIZ UDGothic, Zen Kaku Gothic New, Hina Mincho, Cherry Bomb One, Stick, Murecho, Zen Old Mincho.
